Video quality

Why a Reel can look different after upload.

Updated September 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Resolution is only one part of quality

Two 1080×1920 videos can look very different if one has a much lower bitrate, heavier compression or more aggressive noise reduction. Pixel dimensions alone do not tell the whole story.

Platforms transcode uploads

Large social platforms typically process uploaded videos into delivery-friendly versions. This helps playback across different devices and connections, but the delivered stream may contain less detail than your master export.

Fine texture suffers first

Hair, grass, confetti, low-light noise and fast movement are difficult to compress. These areas may look softer or blockier after platform encoding, especially during rapid motion.

A downloader cannot recreate missing detail

If the public source is already compressed, downloading it at its highest available resolution preserves that delivered version; it cannot restore detail that was removed during earlier encoding.

Best creator workflow

Keep the original export, use a sensible high-quality source, avoid unnecessary edit-and-reupload cycles and treat downloaded platform copies as secondary archives.
