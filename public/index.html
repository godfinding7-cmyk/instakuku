FAQ

Before you save a Reel.

Does InstaKuku work with private Reels?+

No. It is limited to publicly accessible Reel URLs and does not bypass private-account restrictions.

Do I need to log in to Instagram?+

No Instagram username or password is requested by this site.

Why can a public Reel sometimes fail?+

Instagram can rate-limit or block automated requests from hosting-provider IP addresses. A Reel can be public in the app and still be temporarily inaccessible to the server.

Can I repost anything I download?+

No. Downloading a file does not grant copyright or reposting rights. Use content you own or have permission to reuse.

Do policy pages guarantee AdSense approval?+

No. Approval depends on the whole site, including original value, policy compliance, navigation, content depth and traffic quality.
