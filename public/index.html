FAQ

Before you download.

Does this work with private Instagram Reels?

No. InstaKuku is intentionally limited to public Reel URLs and does not provide a private-account or login bypass.

Do I need to enter my Instagram username or password?

No. Never enter your Instagram password into a third-party downloader. This project does not request it.

Can I repost a Reel after downloading it?

Downloading a file does not automatically grant copyright or reposting rights. Use your own content or obtain the creator's permission where required.

Why are some quality options missing?

Available formats depend on what Instagram exposes for that public Reel at the time of analysis. The site cannot create a quality higher than the source it receives.

Does having policy pages guarantee AdSense approval?

No. AdSense approval depends on the whole site, including original value, policy compliance, navigation, traffic quality, content depth and other factors. Policy pages are necessary structure, not a guarantee.
