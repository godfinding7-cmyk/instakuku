Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This template must be reviewed and customized for your actual hosting, analytics, advertising and data practices before launch.

Information you provide

When you use the downloader, you submit an Instagram Reel URL for processing. The site does not require an Instagram username or password.

Server logs

Your hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, referring page and error logs for security and operational purposes.

Temporary media processing

When a download is requested, the server may temporarily store the requested public media file so it can be delivered. The starter backend removes that temporary file after the response completes or after an error cleanup path.

Cookies and advertising

If you later enable Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads subject to their policies. Add the appropriate consent mechanism for jurisdictions where consent is required.

Analytics

No analytics service is configured in this starter. If you add analytics, disclose the provider and data use here.

Data retention

Application-level persistent storage is not configured for submitted Reel URLs in this starter. Hosting logs may be retained according to your host's settings.

Contact

For privacy requests, replace the placeholder contact address on the Contact page with a monitored email.
