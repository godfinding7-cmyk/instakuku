About

A downloader with context.

InstaKuku is designed as a focused utility for people who need to save publicly accessible Instagram Reels they own or are authorized to download. The site deliberately pairs the tool with original educational content so visitors understand quality, copyright and privacy issues around social-media downloads.

Our product principles

Simple interface: the main task should be obvious without forcing users through unnecessary pages or sign-ups.

No password collection: a public-link downloader should not need your Instagram login credentials.

Clear limits: private media and permission barriers are not features to bypass.

Useful content: guides should answer real creator questions rather than exist only as filler around advertisements.

Independence
